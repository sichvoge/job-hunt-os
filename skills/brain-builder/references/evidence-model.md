# Evidence Model

Brain Builder maintains a structured model of what is known about the user's career.

The model must keep **facts**, **interpretations**, **hypotheses**, and **unknowns** distinct.

## Evidence record

A normalized evidence record should represent one coherent achievement, initiative, responsibility, decision, or recurring scope area.

Recommended fields:

```yaml
id: stable-human-readable-id
company: Example Corp
role: Staff Product Manager
period: 2024-2026
summary: Short neutral description of the evidence cluster.

facts:
  - claim: Built a new analytics product from 0 to 1.
    sources:
      - source-id

  - claim: Reached 2,500 monthly active users.
    sources:
      - source-id

interpretations:
  - 0-to-1 product development
  - platform product management

signals:
  - product strategy
  - technical product leadership

domains:
  - analytics
  - developer platforms

confidence: high

gaps:
  - What decision did the user personally own?
  - What alternatives were considered?
```

This schema may evolve as Job Hunt OS learns from real use. Do not add fields merely for theoretical completeness.

## Facts

Facts are source-supported claims about what happened.

Examples:

- shipped a particular product,
- owned a named product area,
- achieved a stated metric,
- presented to named stakeholder levels,
- worked with a specified team size,
- made a decision the user explicitly says they made.

Facts must have provenance.

Do not strengthen a source claim during normalization. For example, "worked with" must not become "led" unless another source supports leadership.

## Interpretations

Interpretations describe what the facts may demonstrate professionally.

Examples:

- platform strategy,
- executive influence,
- commercialization,
- technical depth,
- 0-to-1 product leadership.

Interpretations can be generated by the model but must remain distinguishable from facts.

## Hypotheses

Hypotheses are context-dependent conclusions that should be tested rather than stored as career truth.

Examples:

- this evidence may be especially strong for Staff PM roles,
- the user's strongest market positioning may be observability,
- a hiring manager may see limited evidence of people leadership.

Store hypotheses only when useful, and label them explicitly. Prefer keeping job-specific hypotheses outside the core evidence record when possible.

## Unknowns and gaps

Absence of evidence is not negative evidence.

If the brain does not know the size of a team, record it as unknown or ask the user when that information would materially improve the brain.

Do not translate "unknown team size" into "small team" or "no leadership experience".

## Confidence

Use confidence to describe how well a factual cluster is supported, not how impressive it is.

Suggested values:

- `high`: directly supported by clear user-provided source material or repeated consistent sources,
- `medium`: supported but wording, ownership, scope, or context remains ambiguous,
- `low`: tentative or based on an unclear source and requires confirmation.

Do not use confidence as a substitute for provenance.

## Provenance

Every factual claim must point to at least one source identifier.

A source can be:

- an imported document,
- a resume,
- a user-provided note,
- an interview transcript generated by Brain Builder,
- another explicit user statement preserved as source material.

Preserve enough metadata to identify where the claim came from.

Do not cite model-generated summaries as independent evidence for claims that originated elsewhere.

## Contradictions

When two sources conflict:

1. retain both claims and their provenance,
2. mark the conflict,
3. ask the user to resolve it when material,
4. do not silently choose whichever version appears more favorable.

## Deduplication

Multiple sources may describe the same achievement. Merge them into one evidence cluster when they clearly refer to the same underlying work, while retaining all relevant provenance.

Do not merge separate achievements merely because they demonstrate the same competency.

## Generated output

If a structured evidence file is generated, place a warning at the top indicating that it is generated and should not be manually edited.

Example:

```yaml
# AUTO-GENERATED BY JOB HUNT OS
# Do not edit manually. Update source material through Brain Builder instead.
```

The source material is the durable factual record. The normalized evidence model is a derived representation that may be regenerated.
